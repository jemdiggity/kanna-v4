Tab container — a pane slot that holds panes as tabs

The right half of this window is one tab container, docs (c1), holding four panes. Click its tabs, close them, reorder them by dragging, ⌘-click a link in a document, or press ⌃Tab. The panel on the right shows what kanna list would print.

Keys and gestures

      
    
        
  • Click a tab: focus that pane. Middle-click or ×: close it.
    • 
        
  • ⌃Tab / ⌃⇧Tab: next / previous tab in the focused container.
    • 
        
  • ⌘W: close the active tab's pane; the container closes with its last one.
    • 
        
  • +: a new terminal, file or URL as a tab here.
    • 
        
  • ⌘-click a .md link: open it as a new tab beside this one. A plain click navigates in place, as today.
    • 
        
  • Drag a tab along the strip to reorder; out onto a split edge to take it out; onto another container's strip to move it there.
