Desk: one task per window, the rest on the Shelf

A task is what a Desk holds: its agent, docked on the left, and its tabs. The Desk shows one; the Shelf below holds the project's other tasks, their agents still running. Click a Shelf item to swap, Park to clear the Desk, drag the line between the agent and the tabs, or type a kanna command in the agent's pane as the agent would.
